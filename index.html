About the Festival

Empowering the Next Generation of Creative Talent

The Short Cutz Cr8tv Festival is a multidisciplinary creative and cultural festival designed to discover, develop, celebrate, and promote emerging creative talents while using the power of arts, culture, storytelling, film, media, technology, and entertainment to inspire positive social development.

With Badagry as the host community, the festival will promote its rich cultural heritage, history, tourism potential, and creative identity while positioning it as a hub for creative expression, cultural exchange, and artistic innovation.

Festival Programs

What to Experience

Four days of creativity, learning, and connection.

Film Screenings

Curated screenings of short films and documentaries, followed by conversations with filmmakers and audiences.

Creative Masterclasses

Practical sessions covering filmmaking, directing, cinematography, screenwriting, editing, sound, and more.

Industry Panels

Conversations with professionals addressing opportunities, emerging technologies, monetisation, and career development.

Digital & AI Showcase

Innovative use of AI tools, digital production techniques, animation, visual effects, and new media storytelling.

Talent Showcase

Opportunities for emerging talents to present their work to industry professionals and audiences.

Networking Sessions

Structured meeting points for creators, producers, media organisations, sponsors, and potential collaborators.
